Three steps

From quiet model to conversation.

YapParty is the voice. Your LLM server is the brain. Setup takes a few minutes, once.

Run a local model

Open LM Studio or Ollama and load a model you like. Any OpenAI-compatible server works too.

Open YapParty

Allow the microphone. The first launch downloads about 1.6 GB of speech models, one time.

Start talking

Hold to talk, or go hands-free. YapParty listens, your model replies, and you hear the answer.

Where your voice goes

One turn, start to finish.

Here is everything that happens between you speaking and YapParty answering.

On your Mac

You speak

The mic audio stays in memory. It is never saved to disk.

On your Mac

Transcribed

WhisperKit turns your speech into text, on device.

Your LLM server

Your model replies

The text goes to the server you chose: LM Studio, Ollama or another.

On your Mac

Spoken aloud

Qwen3-TTS or a macOS voice reads the reply, on device.

When your LLM server runs on the same Mac, the whole conversation stays on your Mac. If you point YapParty at a server on another computer or online, your chat messages go to that server.
